Two dedicated Word research reports on the **Klein attractor** program — the dynamical-systems branch of the repository that studies attractor dynamics with ergodic-theoretic structure and builds the bridge back to the Navier–Stokes setting. The two documents are complementary rather than duplicating:

- **`Klein_FAttractor_NS_Research.docx`** (~1.0 MB) — the core research report on the attractor itself: its invariant-measure behaviour and the structural properties that make the dynamics interesting;
- **`Klein_NS_Bridge_Research.docx`** (~1.9 MB) — the bridge study: how the attractor's dynamics connect to the Navier–Stokes framework stabilised by the polarization correction *b*.

These reports correspond to **Section 5** of the verification framework ([`verification/section5_klein_attractor/`](../../verification/section5_klein_attractor/README.md) and its ports in all languages). Unlike the correction-b and KdV programs, no journal PDF is published for this program — the Word reports here are its primary documentary form.
