The **complete figure set of the English v22 monograph** — 19 numbered PNG figures (~5.2 MB total) covering the full evidential chain: b(N) convergence and its bootstrap stability, spacing histograms against the GUE surmise, Σ₂/Δ₃ rigidity statistics, the pair-correlation and form factor (the Montgomery–Dyson shape), Dirac-cone and Dirac-dip structure of the parent lattice, the Byers–Yang flux check, Berry–Robnik cutoff analysis, the Hatano–Nelson drift signature, the γ-phase diagram, the vortex texture, and the Hofstadter butterfly. Figure numbers are consistent across the three language editions (fig18 is intentionally skipped in the numbering).
