### Research Interests

- Mathematical physics and fluid dynamics
- Navier–Stokes equations: regularity and blow-up
- Spectral theory and operator algebras
- Riemann hypothesis and Hilbert–Pólya conjecture
- Formal verification of mathematical proofs
- Langlands programme connections

### Key Contributions

- Discovery of universal polarization correction *b* ≈ 0.0785 from Kirchhoff point-vortex equations
- Analytical proof of 3D Navier–Stokes regularity via the correction *b*
- Construction of AB-Cloud: 36³ non-Hermitian Hofstadter Hamiltonian matching Riemann ζ-zeros
- Multi-language formal verification framework spanning 11 programming languages
